1. What we collect
When you use the Bot, it may process:
• Discord identifiers: user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs
• Command and message content: the text of commands you send to the Bot and, the messages it is configured to read
• Settings you choose: server configuration, preferences
• Basic logs: timestamps and error logs used to keep the Bot running
We do not collect passwords, payment details, or private (DM) conversations unless you message the Bot directly.
2. Why we collect it
We use this data to run the Bot's features, remember your settings, prevent abuse, fix bugs, and improve the Bot. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
3. Who we share it with
We don't share data.
4. How long we keep it
We keep data for as long as the Bot is in your server or you continue to use it. logs are deleted after 14 days. When the Bot is removed from a server, we delete that server's stored settings within 5 days.
5. Your choices and deletion requests
You can ask us to access or delete the data we hold about you or your server by contacting us at haruk0general@gmail.com with your Discord user ID or server ID. Server administrators can also remove the Bot at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR; we will respond to requests within 14 days.
6. Security
We use reasonable measures, such as access controls and encrypted connections, to protect the data we hold. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
7. Children
The Bot is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required to use Discord. We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.
8. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time and will change the "Last updated" date when we do. If the changes are significant, we will announce them in https://discord.gg/hKwUPEnc85.
9. Contact
For privacy questions or requests, contact Haruk0kun at haruk0general@gmail.com
